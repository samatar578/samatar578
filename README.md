## Featured Projects

### 🌍 WildNorth Kenya

**Technology for wildlife conservation.** &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Conservation`

**The Challenge**
Wildlife and habitat protection in northern Kenya depends on local participation, but communities often lack accessible tools to report and share information.

**The Platform**
A conservation initiative combining technology, education, and community engagement to support wildlife reporting, sighting data, and habitat protection.

**Capabilities**
- Wildlife reporting &nbsp; · &nbsp; Wildlife sightings
- Conservation education &nbsp; · &nbsp; Community participation
- Information sharing &nbsp; · &nbsp; Habitat protection

<sub>`React` &nbsp; `JavaScript` &nbsp; `Vite` &nbsp; `Tailwind CSS`</sub>

### 🚨 Horn Watch

**Protecting lives, one alert at a time.** &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Emergency Technology`

**The Challenge**
Emergency information across the Horn of Africa is fragmented, delayed, and often fails to reach the people who need it most.

**The Platform**
A mobile-first system centralizing emergency alerts, disaster reporting, location data, and community–responder coordination in real time.

**Capabilities**
- Emergency alerts &nbsp; · &nbsp; Disaster reporting
- Maps and locations &nbsp; · &nbsp; Community coordination
- Responder management &nbsp; · &nbsp; Multilingual support
- Real-time information

<sub>`React` &nbsp; `JavaScript` &nbsp; `Vite` &nbsp; `Tailwind CSS`</sub>

<br>

<br>

### 🛡️ SafePath Youth Hub

**Protect your mind. Protect your journey.** &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Youth Education`

**The Challenge**
Young people face addiction, peer pressure, mental health challenges, and the risks of irregular migration — often without accessible, judgment-free information.

**The Platform**
A youth awareness hub offering structured educational content on personal development, addiction awareness, peer pressure, and migration risks.

**Capabilities**
- Youth awareness &nbsp; · &nbsp; Addiction awareness
- Peer pressure &nbsp; · &nbsp; Personal development
- Migration awareness &nbsp; · &nbsp; Educational resources

<sub>`HTML` &nbsp; `CSS` &nbsp; `JavaScript`</sub>
